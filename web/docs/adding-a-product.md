# How to add a product to the website

This guide is for the store owner — no technical knowledge needed. It walks
through adding a new product using the admin dashboard.

## Adding a new product

1. Go to **yourdomain.com/admin/login** (replace "yourdomain.com" with the
   real website address) and sign in with your **Username** and **Password**,
   then click **Sign In**.
2. On the Products page, click the **+ Add New Product** button near the
   top right.
3. Fill in the product details:
   - **Product Name** — e.g. "Hand-Painted Silk Cushion"
   - **Description** — a short description of what makes the piece special
   - **Price (PKR)** — the price in Pakistani Rupees
   - Leave the **"This item is available for purchase"** box checked if
     customers should be able to buy it right away (uncheck it if it's out
     of stock or not ready yet)
4. Click **Add Photos** to choose photos from your computer or phone — you
   can select several at once. They'll show up as previews under "Will be
   added when you save."
5. Click **Create Product** at the bottom.

That's it. Your product will appear on the website within about a minute.

## Adding or changing photos on an existing product

1. Sign in and go to the **Products** page.
2. Click **Edit** next to the product you want to change.
3. To add more photos, click **Add Photos** and choose your files — these
   upload right away, no need to click Save.
4. To remove a photo, click the **✕** in its top-right corner and confirm.
5. To reorder photos (the first one is what customers see first), use the
   **↑ Up** / **↓ Down** buttons under each photo.
6. If you changed the name, description, price, or the in-stock checkbox,
   click **Save Changes** at the bottom to save those.

## Marking something as out of stock

You don't need to open the product at all for this:

1. Sign in and go to the **Products** page.
2. Find the product in the list and click the toggle switch in the
   **In Stock** column. It flips between "Available" and "Out of stock"
   immediately.

## Removing a product entirely

1. Sign in and go to the **Products** page.
2. Click **Delete** next to the product.
3. Confirm when asked — this cannot be undone, so make sure it's the right
   product.

## If something goes wrong

- If you can't sign in, double check the username and password. If you've
  forgotten them, you'll need a developer to reset your account from the
  backend (see `backend/README.md`, "Creating an admin/superuser account").
- If a save or upload fails, you'll see a small message on the page — try
  again. If it keeps failing, note what you were doing and contact your
  developer.
